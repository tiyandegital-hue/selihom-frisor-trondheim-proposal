# NICHE — Hair salon / barber (Selihom Frisør, Trondheim, Norway)

**Rule:** the niche's best sites decide the visual language. References from the roundup "25 Modern & Beautiful Hair Salon Websites" (thesalonbusiness.com/hair-salon-websites, updated 2026-04-14, re-checked 2026-11-10) plus one live Trondheim competitor found in search.

## 1. The Harlot Salon — avant-garde salon
- **URL:** https://harlotsalon.com
- **What makes it work:** Bold fullscreen video hero showing the portfolio and the stylists at work — you feel the salon before you read a word. Clean elegant layout in black, white and red. Online booking embedded seamlessly on-site (Mangomint widget, brand-matched). Style + function in balance.

## 2. Palm Sunday — salon + shop
- **URL:** https://palmsunday.co
- **What makes it work:** Clean, minimalist, visually appealing — colour scheme matched to the brand. Booking plus a webshop for salon products in one flow, so the site earns money beyond appointments. Proof a small salon site can sell products without clutter.

## 3. Glasshouse Salon — London, eco salon
- **URL:** https://www.glasshousesalon.co.uk
- **What makes it work:** Minimalist, simple design with impressive use of colour, typography and photo layout. The eco/organic positioning is visible in the first screen — you know what they stand for immediately. Lesson: one clear idea, carried consistently, beats a page of claims.

## 4. Studio Beve — Trondheim (direct local competitor)
- **URL:** https://studiobeve.no/en/barber/
- **What makes it work:** A real Trondheim beauty & barber shop with a proper site: named owner (Evelin Bence), named barbers with bios and backgrounds, clear service menu with prices (e.g. skinfade 790 kr), free parking called out. This is the local bar Selihom must clear — named people + transparent prices + a story. (Also useful: it shows the Trondheim price level — Selihom's 300 kr herreklipp is visibly good value.)

## 5. Björn Axén — Stockholm & Göteborg, Sweden (Scandinavian reference)
- **URL:** via the roundup above (thesalonbusiness.com/hair-salon-websites) — site URL not captured verbatim; do not guess it.
- **What makes it work:** Former Global Salon Business Awards winner. Fullscreen video of glamorous customers, strong Scandinavian fashion-salon polish. The Nordic reference point: restrained, confident, image-led, no shouting.

## Visual direction chosen for Selihom — "FJORD POSTER"
Distilled from the references (esp. Harlot's video-led boldness + Glasshouse's one-idea clarity + Björn Axén's Nordic restraint):
- **Palette:** deep petrol-teal base, ice-white type, signal orange as the single hot accent. NO ivory paper, NO near-black ink + one-accent house template — this is a dark, cold, poster-like system, not a light editorial one.
- **Type:** condensed uppercase display (poster style, e.g. Anton/Oswald-class) for headlines + prices as huge numerals; clean grotesque for body. Norwegian copy throughout.
- **Layout idiom:** poster grid — oversized service names with giant prices, hairline rules, strict columns, big "BESTILL TIME" blocks. Prices are the hero (300 kr herreklipp front and centre — the value story).
- **Motion personality:** sharp and precise — hard cuts, quick slides, no soft fades; a ticking "open now / closed" status line; booking button pulses once, then still.
- **Hero moment:** full-bleed barbershop visual under a giant "SELIHOM FRISØR" condensed headline + "Herreklipp 300 kr — Bestill time" as the first thing a visitor reads. Price-led, not adjective-led.
