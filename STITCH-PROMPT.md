## Design bar — "FJORD POSTER" (fresh visual language, per the Design Variety Law)
- **NEVER use the ivory-paper + near-black-ink + single-accent house template.** This site must look nothing like a generic agency template and nothing like the Queen Nail build (plum/coral) — different palette, different type, different layout.
- **Palette:** deep petrol-teal base, ice-white type, signal orange as the single hot accent. Dark, cold, poster-like.
- **Typography:** condensed uppercase display type (poster style) for headlines; prices set as HUGE numerals; clean grotesque for body copy.
- **Layout idiom:** poster grid — oversized service names with giant prices, hairline rules, strict columns, big "BESTILL TIME" blocks. Prices are the hero: "Herreklipp 300 kr" must be readable in the first screen.
- **Motion personality:** sharp and precise — hard cuts, quick slides, no soft fades. One status line ("Åpent nå / Stengt — åpner 10:00") that updates by the hour. Booking button pulses once, then still.
- **Hero moment:** full-bleed barbershop visual under a giant condensed "SELIHOM FRISØR" headline, with "Herreklipp 300 kr — Bestill time" as the first thing a visitor reads. Price-led, not adjective-led.
- **Psychology first:** a man in Trondheim centre deciding where to get a cut needs three things in 3 seconds — price (300 kr), place (Ravelsveita 4), and the booking button. Everything else is secondary.
